# Contingency test

Infant mobile paradigm on deterministic synthetic fixtures: hand pulses per 100 decisions over 4 seeds, 96 decisions per phase. In the contingent condition a hand pulse changes the next contact; in the yoked condition contact replays the contingent timeline of another seed regardless of action. Standard: a pulse produces contact. Inverted: a pulse removes constant contact. Bimodal: a pulse turns half contact into full or none by a fair coin, leaving the average unchanged. No controller receives a reward.
