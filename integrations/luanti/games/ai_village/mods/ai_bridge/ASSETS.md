# AI Bridge assets

The founder creature is original artwork created for AI Village and is licensed under
CC0-1.0. It is a deliberately simple textured cube assembled in `init.lua` from Luanti's
opaque `ai_bridge_base.png`: moss-green side/top shading and an amber front face make
its heading visible without importing any third-party artwork.

`palette_generated.lua` and `textures/palette.json` are generated data for the original
test-room palette; see the generator for source hashes and regeneration checks.
